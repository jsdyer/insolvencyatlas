Data and definitions

Filing counts. The Office of the Superintendent of Bankruptcy publishes the monthly series by province and territory. Calendar-year views sum twelve reported months. Partial years and incomplete totals are shown as missing.

Annual rates. Consumer rates are per 1,000 residents aged 18 and older. Business rates are per 1,000 businesses. The rate workbook provides separate series for provinces and economic regions, census metro areas, and business sectors. Area definitions and reporting availability can change over time.

Measures. Insolvencies include bankruptcies and proposals. The corporate series is a subset of business filings, which also cover individual businesses.
